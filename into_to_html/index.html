The Saige & Suki Chronicles

Saige is a 10 year old Malinois that hangs out with her 2 year old mini Dachshund sister. They go on adventures. They get into trouble. They love eating snacks

Howloween is their favorite holiday. They enjoy going stick or treating with their moms. The neighbors spoil them with homeade treats

What Saige Does For Fun

Saige likes to play with her Chuck-it ball. Any place. Any time. If you know anthing about the Malinois breed then you know they are obsessed with fetching. She lives for it.

What Suki Does For Fun

Suki is a mini Dachshund. She doesn't play fetch but she loves to play tug o' war with all of her toys. Every toy is her favorite. She's obsessed with real squirrels. One of these days she might catch one.
